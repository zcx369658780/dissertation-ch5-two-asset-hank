## 1. Exact nonlinear iteration state

The minimal mathematical nonlinear state is **only `V_n`**.

For fixed prices, calibration, grids and productivity transitions, the policy
map, selected utility and generator are deterministic derived objects:

`(P_n,u_n,Q_n) = corrected_map(V_n)`.

They must be persisted as durable checkpoint evidence but are not independent
state variables. Carrying them as independent mutable state would permit a
stale-policy or stale-operator update not generated by the current value.

The invariant mass `p_n` is not a nonlinear HJB state. It does not enter the
frozen household HJB, selector, controls, utility or implicit value update.
Including it in every iteration would add expensive KFE solves without changing
the HJB map and would blur an operator diagnostic with a joint fixed point.

For recovery and audit, each completed checkpoint is nevertheless the immutable
tuple `(V_n,P_n,u_n,Q_n,metrics_n)`, with `p_n` present only when a terminal KFE
gate was legally reached.

## 2. Exact update order

For a general checkpoint `V_n`, the authority-supported order is:

1. Hash-bind finite `V_n`, exact `(20,20,2)` shape, F-order, call-725 grid,
   prices, calibration, productivity generator and frozen scientific code.
2. Construct raw forward/backward one-sided derivatives from `V_n`. At an asset
   face duplicate the valid inward derivative only into the unused finite carrier
   slot and persist `UNUSED_BOUNDARY_SLOT_DUPLICATES_INWARD_RAW_DERIVATIVE`.
   The duplicate is not a candidate.
3. Traverse all 800 cells in F-order, b fastest. Call the accepted corrected
   selector once per cell under one shared prospective budget. Apply unchanged
   D1/D3, lower-a zero-kink and interior-Z laws. Persist each complete comparison
   receipt before advancing.
4. Only after 800/800 `SELECTED_ADMISSIBLE`, assemble `Q_n` once from consumed
   total drifts using unchanged D2. Enforce strict zero-tolerance closed faces,
   nonnegative offdiagonals, exact diagonal construction, `Q_n @ 1`, coordinate
   action and durable receipt checks.
5. With `P_n,u_n,Q_n` generated from the same `V_n`, compute and persist the
   stationary Bellman residual

   `R_n = rho*vec_F(V_n) - vec_F(u_n) - Q_n*vec_F(V_n)`.

   Also compute value-change, policy-stability and operator-stability metrics
   against the prior checkpoint where available.
6. If and only if the future Owner-bound convergence law passes, do not perform
   another HJB update. Proceed to the terminal topology/KFE gate for this exact
   `Q_n`.
7. Otherwise form, with fixed `Delta=1000`,

   `M_n = (rho + 1/Delta)*I_800 - Q_n`,

   `rhs_n = vec_F(u_n) + vec_F(V_n)/Delta`,

   and perform one sparse direct solve

   `vec_F(V_(n+1)) = solve(M_n,rhs_n)`.

8. Persist the original 800 equations' residual, normwise backward error,
   `V_(n+1)` hash, `V_(n+1)-V_n` hash/norm and the complete round ledger before
   beginning any derivative construction at `n+1`.

The implicit direct-solve residual is evidence that the linearized false-
transient step was solved numerically. It is not the stationary Bellman residual
and cannot establish nonlinear convergence.

## 3. KFE timing

KFE must be executed **only after HJB convergence**, not every iteration.

This follows from the frozen equations, not convenience: under fixed call-725
prices and calibration, `p_n` has no feedback into `V_(n+1)`, policies or `Q_n`.
An intermediate KFE therefore cannot change the HJB path. The economically
relevant forward equation is tied to the final same-value operator `Q*`.

At the HJB convergence candidate, perform exactly one exact-positive graph/SCC
audit. Require one closed communicating class before the pin-free KFE solve.
Then apply the accepted source-free contract to the same `Q*`:

- `Q*.T @ p* = 0`;
- `(b,a,z)=(20,20,2)`, F-order, b fastest;
- `omega=70/361`, probability mass `p*`, density `g*=p*/omega`;
- no row replacement, pin, RHS source or balancing source;
- one full dense `scipy.linalg.svd(..., lapack_driver="gesvd")`;
- one global sign orientation and one normalization;
- rank/nullity `799/1`, one closed class, stationarity, normalization and
  nonnegative-mass checks under the accepted prospective arithmetic formulas.

An intermediate topology summary may be persisted each round. Multiple closed
classes in an intermediate `Q_n` are not by themselves proof that HJB continuation
cannot later change topology; the accepted Q0-to-Q1 transition already demonstrates
that topology can change after a value update. Multiple closed classes in the
**terminal same-value `Q*`** are a joint-fixed-point failure and stop before SVD.

## 4. Convergence metrics and their roles

Four distinct metrics must not be conflated:

1. **Bellman residual** `||R_n||_inf` tests the stationary HJB equation at the
   same `V_n,P_n,u_n,Q_n`. It is the primary equation-level convergence evidence.
2. **Value change** `||V_n-V_(n-1)||_inf` measures the false-transient algorithm's
   step size. It guards against declaring convergence from a single small
   equation residual or a numerical accident, but is not itself the HJB equation.
3. **Policy stability** records exact selected branch/active-set/transfer/Z-marker
   identity and changes in continuous controls/drifts. It diagnoses selector
   chatter or switching. Exact branch stability does not prove the Bellman equation,
   and continuous policies require an authority-backed scale if approximate
   stability is made terminal.
4. **Operator stability** records CSR sparsity identity, numeric field hashes and
   a prospective norm of `Q_n-Q_(n-1)`. It detects topology/operator chatter but
   does not replace the Bellman residual.

Repository authority does not determine:

- absolute versus scale-normalized Bellman residual;
- exact `tau_Bellman` and `tau_value`;
- strict `<` versus inclusive `<=` at those thresholds;
- whether policy/operator stability is mandatory or diagnostic only;
- if mandatory, their continuous-field norms, thresholds and consecutive-round
  window;
- a prospective direct-linear-solve backward-error rejection threshold;
- a quantitative non-exact oscillation rule.

The historical strict `value change < 1e-7` cannot silently fill these gaps. A
future law must be frozen before its first scientific call and may not be tuned
after observing a trajectory.

## 5. Joint HJB-KFE fixed-point termination

A terminal household fixed-point claim requires all of the following from one
same-value checkpoint:

1. `V*` passes the Owner-bound Bellman-residual and algorithmic convergence law.
2. `P*,u*,Q*` were freshly and completely generated from `V*` under the frozen
   corrected selector/D2 laws; no stale policy/operator is used.
3. Any Owner-required policy/operator stability guards pass.
4. `Q*` passes D2 construction and has exactly one closed communicating class.
5. One pin-free homogeneous KFE validation returns unique `p*` satisfying
   `Q*.T @ p*=0`, normalization and nonnegativity within frozen arithmetic bounds.
6. Every object is finite, hash-bound and in the same F-order state convention.

This is a stationary **household HJB-KFE checkpoint conditional on frozen prices
and calibration**. It is not GE or a production/paper stationary equilibrium;
market clearing, price consistency and outer fixed points remain outside scope.

convergence law, all selected-policy legality, D2 validity, finite direct-solve
history, no forbidden numerical device and complete durable trajectory evidence.

Requires only an identified finite Q plus the accepted source-free structural,
rank/nullity, stationarity, normalization and nonnegative-mass contract. Q1/p1
already has this status. It does not imply HJB convergence.

`P*,u*,Q*` derived from `V*` and `Q*.T@p*=0`. It remains conditional on fixed
prices/calibration and is not GE, production replacement or Results authority.
